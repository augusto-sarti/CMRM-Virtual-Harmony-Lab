3. Major versus minor II–V–I: hear the voice leading

The chord symbols are realized as smooth four-part teaching voicings. The dominant omits its fifth so that root, 3rd and 7th — and their melodic resolutions — are easy to hear.

Listen first to the full cadence, then isolate one line. The harmonic change is also a set of melodic motions distributed across the voices.

What changed?

  • the reference collection: C major → C harmonic minor
  • E became E♭ and A became A♭
  • ii became half-diminished
  • the tonic quality changed from major to minor
  • optional A♭/B colour tones can make harmonic-minor tension more explicit

What remained invariant?

  • the roots D → G → C
  • the functional trajectory ii → V → I/i
  • the G7 dominant core
  • dominant resolution toward C
