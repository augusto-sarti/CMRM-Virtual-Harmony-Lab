# CMRM Harmony Lab Compendium v0.14

Labs 1–6 are retained. Lab 7 has been revised again after listening tests, with two specific goals: make the three playback modes audibly distinct, and reconstruct the reharmonized passage as genuinely continuous melodic voices.

## Lab 7 — Reharmonization
- Keeps the eight-note soprano phrase invariant.
- Compares a plain functional path, functional-family substitutions, and a reharmonized path.
- Uses C7 to tonicize IV and Fm6 as modal mixture in the reharmonized path.
- Shows the chordal role of every fixed melody note.
- Includes a step-by-step construction of the reharmonization.
- Uses exact-note local piano WAV playback with no runtime transposition.

## v0.14 listening / voice-leading correction
- `Play melody + harmony` now plays four accompaniment voices plus the fixed soprano as a separately balanced upper line.
- `Harmony only` now truly removes the soprano; it no longer reproduces the same top melody as part of the chord object.
- `A/B` is disabled while version A is selected and, for B or C, plays baseline A, a clear pause, then the selected version.
- Long piano samples are gated so successive notes do not blur into one another during analytic listening.
- The three harmonizations have been revoiced as five-part realizations (four support voices + fixed soprano).
- Section 5 uses a globally voice-led realization of version C with very small motions in all four lower parts. Inversions are permitted explicitly to obtain melodic continuity; harmonic identity remains unchanged.
