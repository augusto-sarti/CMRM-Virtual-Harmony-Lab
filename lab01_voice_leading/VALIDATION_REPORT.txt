CMRM Harmony Lab — Module 1 validation summary

Harmonic target:
Dm7    D F A C
G7     G B D F
Cmaj7  C E G B

Audio inventory used by the module:
MIDI 43 45 47 48 50 52 53 55 57 59 60 62

Design safeguards:
- no HTMLAudioElement.playbackRate transposition
- one exact local WAV per permitted MIDI note
- curated voicings validated against chord pitch classes and sample inventory
- random generator draws only from the verified sample inventory
- random voicings contain one instance of each chord pitch class
- voices are sorted to avoid crossing
- adjacent spacing is constrained to <= 12 semitones
- total voicing span is constrained to <= 19 semitones
- transition selection penalizes large individual leaps
- previous chord audio is stopped before the next chord begins

Pedagogical guide-tone paths are extracted from the selected voicing itself:
F -> F -> E
C -> B -> B
