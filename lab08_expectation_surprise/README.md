# Lab 8 — Harmonic Expectation & Surprise (v0.17)

Expanded from the corrected v0.16 four-voice engine. The lab now restores a full pedagogical arc: expectation-building through isolated voice playback; committed prediction; contextual and pairwise continuation comparisons; qualitative ranking; large and miniature voice-trajectory graphics; an explicit comparison of harmonic function, root motion, modal interpretation, and semitone motion; automatically normalized student probabilities; and a mini prediction experiment comparing the student's model with voice-leading-only and tonal-function toy models using cumulative information-theoretic surprise.

The tonal-function distribution is explicitly illustrative rather than corpus-derived. Its purpose is to demonstrate that different representations generate different predictive distributions.
