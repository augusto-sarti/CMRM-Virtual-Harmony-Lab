8. Harmonic Expectation & Surprise

A tonal context creates expectations because harmony and individual voices both appear to be going somewhere. This lab first lets you hear and inspect those trajectories, then turns your own predictions into a simple computational model.

How can the same context support several continuations, yet make them feel unequally expected?

5. Why do the endings feel different?

Smoothness is only one cue. Compare harmonic function, root destination, modal interpretation and voice motion.

Key idea: a continuation can have economical voice leading and still be unexpected because expectation is learned from tonal syntax and style, not from semitone distance alone.

6. Turn your own expectation into a probability model

Move the sliders as relative weights. The app normalizes them automatically; no total of 100 is required.

What remained invariant?

  • the complete Cmaj7–Am7–Dm7–G7 context
